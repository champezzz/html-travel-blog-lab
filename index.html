Adventures in Fire Island

My trip to Fire Island was an unforgettable experience. I explored beautiful beaches, walked along scenic boardwalks, and visited the historic lighthouse. The park offers stunning coastal views, peaceful nature trails, and a relaxing atmosphere that makes it a perfect destination for outdoor enthusiasts.
